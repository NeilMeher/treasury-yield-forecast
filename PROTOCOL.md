# Research protocol

Frozen 2026-11-18 after inspecting the Fed file and zero-coupon fields. Use January 1985–December 2025, last complete curve per month, continuously compounded Treasury zero yields at 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 and 10 years. Missing daily curves are skipped, and a missing calendar month stops the run. Treat yields as fitted staff estimates. This is a latest-download analysis with no real-time vintage claim. Initial training ends December 1999. Each following monthly origin refits expanding data up to and including that origin. Forecast 1, 3 and 12 months. Use common realizations within each maturity/horizon, with no earlier release claims.

Compare no change, an intercept AR(1) by maturity, and Dynamic Nelson–Siegel using unweighted cross-sectional least squares and separate intercept AR(1) regressions on the factors. Use the published Diebold–Li decay of 0.0609 per month with maturity in months, preselected rather than tuned. Iterate AR(1) for each horizon. Freeze 3-month maturity-pooled RMSE (basis points) as primary. Also report maturity-level RMSE and its ratio to no change. For uncertainty, form paired squared-error gaps and percentile intervals from 1,000 circular blocks of length 12 months per resample, seed 707. This length respects 12-step overlapping forecasts and provides a common diagnostic across horizons. Report targets in Sep. 2008–Jun. 2009, Mar.–Dec. 2020 and 2022 separately. No economic or causal claims.
